Start with your website. Audit technical SEO, inspect competitor sites, generate marketing drafts, and organise leads. This MVP does not yet buy ads, publish posts, send emails, or render AI videos automatically.

1. Business intelligence · Website deep-dive

Checks the homepage and a small sample of internal pages, plus robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Only enter a public website you own or have permission to audit.

2. Competitor comparison

Run a basic public-page audit on a competitor website and compare its title, description, content and checks with your own. Add only one URL at a time.

3. Campaign content generator

Creates editable draft copy for LinkedIn, Facebook, and a short video ad script. It does not render video or publish content.

4. Lead tracker

Save companies you discover so you can track them. This is a tracker, not an automatic lead-finding or email-sending engine.

5. Saved audit history

Connect Cloudflare D1 and run the included database schema to save reports and leads between sessions.
